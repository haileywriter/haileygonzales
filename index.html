Book 2: House of Embers

My fated mate betrayed me.
A five hundred-year-old fae warlord is trying to kill me.
And now I'm a princess.

After unintentionally revealing myself to have royal blood and hatching three baby dragons, the king wants me dead.

I'd run if he didn't have my sister. And since the magical barrier keeping a mysterious warlord known as 'the Great Evil' is failing, I have no choice but to fight alongside the king.

But I won't take this lying down. I'll find a way to solve all my problems, even if I have to sacrifice the world to do it.

A Throne of Smoke and Ruin is a steamy, dragon rider romantasy that includes morally gray characters, enemies-to-lovers, political intrigue, a shadow daddy, and touch her and unalive.
